Why the standings change

Consistent scoring vs. favorable schedules

H2H

Each scheduled matchup is worth one win, loss or tie. Opponent luck affects the result.

H2H + league median

Each week adds a second game: above the weekly median earns a win; below earns a loss. Exact median ties count as half a win.

All-play

Each team is compared against every other team's score that week. A 12-team league creates 11 potential wins per week.

Total points

Standings ignore the matchup schedule and rank teams strictly by accumulated fantasy points.

Current prototype assumptions

One matchup per team per week, no byes or doubleheaders, completed weeks only. Equal standings records break on points for. Adjust division/playoff tiebreakers before any league vote.
